# Legal and Ethics

The framework extracts design decisions. It does not grant rights to the source.

## Authorisation

Before auditing:

- confirm you are authorised to inspect the repository or site;
- confirm whether screenshots, computed styles, and asset metadata may be retained;
- confirm whether the audit is internal, client-facing, or public;
- record the permission and its scope in Phase 0.

If authorisation is unclear, stop.

## Copyright

Protectable expression includes source code, copy, photography, illustration, logos, icon sets, typefaces, and unique layouts.

You may extract and record:

- measurements;
- colour values;
- type sizes and spacing;
- component anatomy;
- interaction patterns;
- information architecture;
- design principles;
- public factual content needed for the audit.

You may not:

- copy source code into the generated skill;
- bundle client photography or illustrations;
- reproduce long passages of copy;
- redistribute a proprietary font;
- re-create a logo or trademark;
- publish a pixel-for-pixel clone.

Write new code and new copy that applies the principles. If a pattern is distinctive and protectable, use it as inspiration, not as a template.

## Trademarks

Do not reproduce a client's brand mark in a generated template unless the client owns the output and has approved it. Do not imply endorsement, partnership, or certification that does not exist.

## Photography and imagery

- Use only client-supplied or licensed assets.
- Record the licence and credit for every image.
- Do not download images from the live site into the generated skill.
- Do not use a generic image to imply a specific place.
- Resolve `TODO confirm credit` before publishing.
- Remove EXIF and GPS data when processing assets, unless the client requires it.

## Fonts

- Record the font licence.
- Do not bundle a font you cannot redistribute.
- Provide fallback stacks.
- If the source uses a commercial font without a web licence, flag it as a P0.

## Third-party code and components

- Respect open-source licences.
- Do not copy a component library's source into the generated skill.
- Record dependencies and their licences.
- Check that icons, illustrations, and motion libraries are licensed for the client's use.

## Privacy

- Do not include personal data in the audit.
- Redact names, emails, phone numbers, and addresses unless they are public business contact details.
- Do not include secrets, tokens, or environment variables.
- Do not retain authenticated screenshots beyond the agreed scope.
- Follow GDPR, CCPA, and local privacy requirements when handling customer data.

## AI and generated content

- Do not present generated copy as client-authored fact.
- Mark assumptions and content slots.
- Do not invent reviews, awards, certifications, prices, availability, or guarantees.
- Keep a human review step for regulated claims.

## No-clone rule

A generated page must be recognisably in the brand's system without being a copy of an existing page. Reuse the tokens and grammar. Write new composition and copy. Do not reproduce unique illustrations, proprietary layouts, or trademarked visual devices.

## Provenance

Every generated skill records:

- source repository or URL;
- commit or capture timestamp;
- audit date and auditor;
- framework and schema versions;
- licence and restrictions;
- evidence count;
- known gaps;
- unresolved P0 issues.

## Redistribution

If the generated skill will be shared:

- remove client-confidential information;
- confirm the client permits derivative design documentation;
- keep the provenance record;
- do not include assets;
- include the licence terms of the framework itself.

## Ethical findings

Report honestly:

- dark patterns;
- fake urgency;
- misleading availability;
- inaccessible flows;
- unlicensed assets;
- privacy risks.

A premium design system is not premium if it is dishonest.
